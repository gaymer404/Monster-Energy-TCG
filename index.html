How it works

Four quick steps from fridge to collection.

1

Scan

Aim your camera at the barcode on the can.

2

Identify

The barcode is matched to a flavor. Unknown cans are linked once, then remembered.

3

Generate

A card is created from the flavor's data. Same flavor, same card, every time.

4

Collect

It lands in your collection. Scan the same flavor again to stack duplicates.

Built for collectors

Simple, fast and entirely yours.

Instant barcode scan

Fast, reliable scanning with your phone's camera.

Deterministic cards

No random re-rolls. A flavor always produces the same card.

Local collection

Your collection is stored on your device. No account, no backend.

Rarity tiers

From Common to Legendary, based on the product line.

Duplicate tracking

See how many copies of each card you own.

Photo recognition

Planned: recognize a can from a photo, no barcode needed.
